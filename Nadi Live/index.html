Our five-step research process

The same discipline we use for our own money. A stock reaches the pool only after it passes every step.

01 · Screen

Profit in each of the last five years? Do we understand how it makes money? Not on our red list?

02 · Analyse

Five years of EPS, book value, ROE and P/E, plus CAR and bad loans for banks.

03 · Watch

A score out of 100, an intrinsic value and a buy-below price. We wait for the price.

04 · Invest

Buy with a stop-loss and a position limit, so no single stock dominates.

05 · Review

Every month: did we follow our rules? Best decision, biggest mistake, one lesson.
